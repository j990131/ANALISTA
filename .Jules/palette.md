## 2024-05-18 - Accessibility for Dynamic Carousels
**Learning:** Carousels that dynamically render products need more than just interactive visual dots. They need a `role="tablist"` on the container and `role="tab"` on the indicators with dynamic `aria-selected` and specific `aria-label`s to inform screen reader users of their current location within the carousel. In addition, icon-only navigation buttons must have `aria-label`s, and live-updating product info containers should have `aria-live="polite"` so screen readers announce changes.
**Action:** When implementing carousels or image galleries, always use the `tablist`/`tab` pattern with `aria-selected` mapping to the active index. Use `aria-live` for sections that update dynamically on navigation, and add `aria-label` to icon-only controls.
