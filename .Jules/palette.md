## 2024-09-13 - Custom Carousel Accessibility
**Learning:** Custom carousels often lack native semantic meaning. Explicit `role="region"`, `aria-roledescription="carousel"` for the stage, and `role="group"`, `aria-roledescription="slide"` for products must be added.
**Action:** Always verify custom carousels and apply proper ARIA roles and descriptions to structure them appropriately for screen reader support.
