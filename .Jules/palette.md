## 2024-05-15 - Dynamic UI Screen Reader Enhancements
**Learning:** In highly visual and dynamic components like the Dental Promo carousel, visual cues for active items (like expanding dots or changing colors) are completely lost on screen readers. Simply adding buttons is not enough. The `role="tab"` and `aria-selected` attributes are necessary to convey pagination state. Moreover, when product text updates dynamically without a page reload, screen readers remain silent unless the container has an `aria-live="polite"` attribute to announce the changes.
**Action:** When implementing custom carousels or dynamically updating product info, always apply `aria-live="polite"` to the text container and use `role="tab"` with `aria-selected` on the pagination indicators to ensure the visual state changes are communicated to all users.
