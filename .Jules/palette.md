## 2024-06-26 - Dental Promo Carousel Accessibility
**Learning:** Carousels in this design system consistently use `.dots` containers which initially lacked screen reader context (`role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`) and dynamically mapped dot buttons missed `aria-selected` tracking, making it impossible for screen readers to know which slide was active. Interactive product cards also failed to have focus states (`tabIndex={0}`) or keyboard handlers (`onKeyDown`), effectively isolating keyboard users from the core interactive experience of previewing different products.
**Action:** Always add `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"` and explicitly track `aria-selected` on custom pagination indicators. Ensure interactive custom elements (like product cards used as large buttons) receive `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, and `Enter`/`Space` key handlers to map to their click actions. Remember to also announce dynamically changing text (like product titles and descriptions below the carousel) using `aria-live="polite"`.
