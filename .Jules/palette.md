## 2024-09-21 - Add ARIA labels to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Custom carousels often use icon-only elements (arrows, dots) for navigation. Without ARIA labels, these controls are completely inaccessible to screen reader users, lacking context for their functionality.
**Action:** Always verify custom interactive controls like carousel arrows and pagination dots have descriptive `aria-label` attributes (e.g., "Previous product", "Product 1").
