## 2024-10-06 - Accessible Carousels
**Learning:** Custom carousel components in the project should use role='region' and aria-roledescription='carousel' on the stage container, and role='group' with aria-roledescription='slide' on the individual product cards for optimal screen reader support.
**Action:** Always apply the appropriate ARIA roles and roledescriptions to custom carousels and their child slides to ensure screen reader users can navigate them effectively.
